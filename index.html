About English Test

Our comprehensive English placement test is designed to accurately assess your language proficiency across all four key skills: listening, reading, speaking, and writing. Based on the internationally recognized CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference) levels, this test provides reliable results to help you understand your current English level and identify areas for improvement.

Why Our Test is Accurate

  • ✓ Expert-Designed Assessment: Carefully crafted questions by English language experts
  • ✓ Real-World Scenarios: Questions based on actual English usage patterns
  • ✓ CEFR Standards: Internationally recognized framework ensures accuracy
  • ✓ Instant Results: Get immediate feedback on your English level

"The limits of my language mean the limits of my world." - Ludwig Wittgenstein

Test Features:

  • Listening Comprehension - Real conversations and audio clips
  • Reading Assessment - Various text types and difficulty levels
  • Speaking Evaluation - Voice recording and pronunciation analysis
  • Writing Skills - Structured writing tasks and grammar assessment

The test takes approximately 15-20 minutes to complete and provides instant results with detailed feedback and recommendations for further learning.
